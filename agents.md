# AGENTS.md

## Project

This repo is for **AJ Digital Services**, a small local tech-help business website.

The site should feel trustworthy, friendly, local, and practical — aimed mainly at:

- Older people
- Families
- Non-technical users
- Local small businesses
- People who need patient help with everyday tech

Avoid making it feel like a corporate SaaS startup.

## Business Summary

AJ Digital Services provides local help with:

- Computers and laptops
- Phone and tablet setup
- WiFi, printers, smart TVs, email, and accounts
- Basic repairs, upgrades, and troubleshooting
- Small business websites and online setup

Tone: clear, friendly, calm, lightly humorous where appropriate.

Main message:
“Friendly local tech help without the confusing jargon.”

## Brand Direction

Use a modern but approachable style.

Preferred feel:

- Clean
- Trustworthy
- Local
- Slightly techy
- Not intimidating
- Easy to read for older users

Avoid:

- Overly futuristic/cyberpunk styling
- Dense text
- Tiny fonts
- Aggressive sales copy
- Too much animation
- Corporate buzzwords

## Tech Stack

Assume:

- Vite
- React
- Tailwind CSS
- Cloudflare Pages hosting

Keep the project lightweight and easy to maintain.

## Development Rules

- Keep components simple and readable.
- Prefer semantic HTML.
- Prioritise accessibility and mobile responsiveness.
- Use clear section names and predictable file structure.
- Do not add unnecessary dependencies.
- Do not over-engineer.
- Do not remove existing content unless asked.
- Keep copy UK-focused.
- Use GBP pricing.
- Use British spelling.

## Website Structure

Start as a one-page website with sections:

1. Hero
2. Services
3. Pricing
4. Why Choose AJ Digital Services
5. About
6. Contact

Later pages may include:

- Services
- Pricing
- Blog
- About
- Contact

## Copywriting Style

Write in plain English.

Good examples:

- “I can help set up your new phone, laptop, printer, WiFi, or smart TV.”
- “No jargon, no pressure — just patient help with everyday technology.”
- “Ideal for older users, families, and small local businesses.”

Avoid:

- “Digital transformation solutions”
- “Enterprise-grade innovation”
- “Synergistic technology enablement”

## Important User Context

The owner, Adam Johnston, has over a decade of informal experience helping friends, family, older people, and local users with tech problems.

He has a BSc in Web Design & Development and experience with websites, repairs, setups, troubleshooting, and explaining technology clearly.

Incoming calls and enquiries may be handled by a booking assistant to reduce pressure and keep things organised.

## Pricing Guidance

Use “from” pricing rather than fixed promises.

Example pricing:

- Home tech visit: from £35
- Extra time: £25/hour
- Device setup: from £40
- PC/laptop health check: from £50
- Small business website: from £300

Always make pricing feel transparent but flexible.

## Contact CTA

Primary CTA:
“Book Tech Help”

Secondary CTA:
“View Services”

Contact wording should make it clear that people can call, text, or send a message.

## Quality Checklist

Before finishing a task, check:

- Does it look trustworthy?
- Is it readable for older users?
- Is it mobile friendly?
- Is the copy clear and not too techy?
- Does it encourage enquiries without sounding pushy?
- Is it simple enough to launch quickly?
